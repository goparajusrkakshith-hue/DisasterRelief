# Disaster Relief Resource Management System

A centralized database engineering project designed to streamline emergency response and relief operations during natural disasters like floods, earthquakes, cyclones, and landslides. The system enables real-time coordination, efficient victim tracking, equitable resource distribution, and seamless volunteer and shelter management to minimize delays and save lives.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Traditional disaster management often relies on manual logs or fragmented systems, leading to misallocation of critical supplies and delays in rescue efforts. This system unifies all emergency management workflows into a single database platform to serve government agencies, NGOs, rescue teams, and disaster victims.

---

## 🔄 End-to-End Workflow

1. **Disaster Occurs & Reported** — Initial incident data and location are logged.


2. **Details Updated** — Real-time assessment of required aid (food, water, medicine, shelters).


3. **Victims Registered** — Affected individuals and families are cataloged for aid distribution.


4. **Resources Allocated** — Supplies are tracked and dispatched accurately.


5. **Volunteers & Rescue Teams Assigned** — Personnel are directed to high-priority areas.


6. **Report Generation** — Final reporting for government oversight and institutional auditing.



---

## 🛠️ System Modules & Features

* **Disaster Management:** Real-time logging of disaster locations, severity, and critical requirements.


* **Victim Tracking:** Registration and status monitoring of affected individuals.


* **Resource & Supply Allocation:** Live inventory management for food, water, medical supplies, and essential goods.


* **Shelter Management:** Occupancy tracking and emergency shelter capacity monitoring.


* **Volunteer & Rescue Team Management:** Dynamic assignment of personnel and field operational support.
